## Getting Started

The Announcements Carousel displays important news, celebrations, and updates on your SharePoint site. Each announcement appears one at a time and automatically rotates to the next.

### Where to Find the List

1. Go to your SharePoint site
2. Click **"Site Contents"** from the gear menu or left navigation
3. Look for the list named **"Announcements Carousel"**
4. Click to open it

## Creating Your First Announcement

### Step 1: Add a New Item

1. Open the "Announcements Carousel" list
2. Click the **"+ New"** button at the top
3. A form will appear with several fields

### Step 2: Fill in the Details

**Required Fields** (marked with *):
- **Title** *: A catchy headline for your announcement
- **Valid From** *: The date when the announcement should start showing
- **Valid To** *: The date when the announcement should stop showing

**Optional Fields**:
- **Description**: Detailed information about the announcement
- **Announcement Image**: URL to an image
- **Celebration Icon**: Choose a special icon to overlay on the image
- **Celebration Icon Position**: Where to place the celebration icon

### Step 3: Save

Click **"Save"** at the bottom of the form. Your announcement will appear in the carousel if today's date is between the Valid From and Valid To dates.

## Field Descriptions

### Title (Required)
**What it is**: The main headline of your announcement

**Tips**:
- Keep it short and catchy (40-60 characters)
- Use action words when appropriate
- Make it clear and descriptive

**Examples**:
- ✅ "Welcome Our New Team Member, Sarah!"
- ✅ "Q4 Sales Target Exceeded by 25%"
- ✅ "Holiday Office Closure - December 24-26"
- ❌ "Announcement" (too vague)

### Description (Optional)
**What it is**: Detailed information about the announcement

**Tips**:
- Use the rich text editor for formatting
- Break content into short paragraphs
- Use bullet points for lists
- Add links to relevant resources
- Keep it concise (2-3 paragraphs max)

**Example**:
```
Please join us in welcoming Sarah Johnson to the Marketing team!

Sarah brings 8 years of experience in digital marketing and will be leading our social media strategy. She starts on Monday, March 15th.

Stop by her desk to say hello!
```

### Announcement Image (Optional)
**What it is**: A URL to an image that displays with your announcement

**How to add an image**:

1. **Upload to SharePoint** (Recommended):
   - Go to a document library (or create one called "Announcement Images")
   - Upload your image
   - Click the "..." menu on the image
   - Select "Details"
   - Copy the "Path" URL
   - Paste this URL into the Announcement Image field

2. **Use an existing URL**:
   - Make sure the image is publicly accessible
   - Copy the full URL
   - Paste into the Announcement Image field

**Image Guidelines**:
- **Size**: 1200 x 600 pixels (2:1 ratio works best)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading
- **Content**: Use high-quality, professional images
- **Accessibility**: Ensure images are clear and easy to understand

### Valid From (Required)
**What it is**: The date and time when your announcement should start appearing

**Tips**:
- Set to today's date for immediate display
- Set to a future date to schedule in advance
- Include time if you want precision (otherwise defaults to midnight)

**Examples**:
- Immediate: Today's date
- Scheduled: Next Monday's date
- Timed: "3/15/2024 9:00 AM"

### Valid To (Required)
**What it is**: The date and time when your announcement should stop appearing

**Tips**:
- Typical duration: 1-4 weeks
- Important news: 2-3 weeks
- Time-sensitive: 3-7 days
- Evergreen content: 1-3 months
- Consider your announcement volume

**Important**: Announcements automatically disappear after this date. You can always edit the item to extend the date.

### Celebration Icon (Optional)
**What it is**: A special emoji icon that overlays on your image

**Available Options**:
- **None**: No icon (default)
- **Birthday 🎂**: For birthday celebrations
- **Anniversary 🎊**: For work anniversaries or company milestones
- **Achievement 🏆**: For awards, goals reached, certifications
- **Celebration 🎉**: For general celebrations and good news
- **New Hire 👋**: For welcoming new team members
- **Promotion ⭐**: For promotions and role changes
- **Holiday 🎄**: For holiday greetings
- **Custom ✨**: For special occasions not covered above

**When to use**:
- Personal celebrations (birthdays, anniversaries)
- Team achievements
- Company milestones
- Holiday greetings
- Special recognitions

**When NOT to use**:
- Standard business announcements
- Policy updates
- General information
- Serious or somber topics

### Celebration Icon Position (Optional)
**What it is**: Where the celebration icon appears on the image

**Options**:
- **Top-Right**: Most common, doesn't obstruct main content
- **Top-Left**: Alternative corner placement
- **Bottom-Right**: Good for images with top-focused content
- **Bottom-Left**: Less common, use sparingly
- **Center**: Makes the icon the focal point (use for big celebrations)

**Default**: Top-Right (if not specified)

## Best Practices

### Writing Effective Announcements

1. **Be Clear and Concise**
   - Get to the point quickly
   - Use simple language
   - Avoid jargon

2. **Include a Call to Action**
   - Tell readers what to do next
   - Examples: "Register here", "Reply by Friday", "Visit the new portal"

3. **Use Appropriate Tone**
   - Match the tone to the content
   - Professional for business updates
   - Warm and friendly for celebrations
   - Urgent for time-sensitive information

### Image Selection

1. **Choose Relevant Images**
   - Image should relate to the announcement
   - Use team photos for people-focused announcements
   - Use professional stock photos for business updates

2. **Quality Matters**
   - Use high-resolution images
   - Avoid pixelated or blurry images
   - Ensure good lighting and composition

3. **Consider Diversity and Inclusion**
   - Use diverse images when possible
   - Be mindful of cultural sensitivity
   - Avoid stereotypes

### Timing Your Announcements

1. **Scheduling**
   - Post at the start of the workday for maximum visibility
   - Allow 1-2 weeks for non-urgent announcements
   - Use shorter durations (3-5 days) for time-sensitive items

2. **Avoid Overlap Overload**
   - Check existing announcements before adding new ones
   - Too many active announcements dilute their impact
   - Aim for 3-5 active announcements maximum

3. **Plan Ahead**
   - Schedule holiday greetings in advance
   - Prepare birthday announcements at the start of the month
   - Create recurring announcements (monthly newsletter, weekly tips)

## Tips and Tricks

### Quick Tips

1. **Preview Before Saving**: Always review your announcement after saving to ensure it looks right

2. **Update Instead of Delete**: If information changes, edit the existing announcement rather than deleting and creating a new one

3. **Use Rich Formatting**:
   - **Bold** important points
   - Use bullet lists for multiple items
   - Add links to relevant resources

4. **Mobile-Friendly**: Keep titles and descriptions readable on small screens

5. **Archive Old Content**: Periodically remove outdated announcements to keep the list manageable

### Creating an Image Library

For easier image management:

1. Create a document library called "Announcement Images"
2. Organize images in folders by month or category
3. Name images descriptively (e.g., "birthday-sarah-march-2024.jpg")
4. Keep a folder of reusable stock images

### Batch Creating Announcements

If you have multiple announcements to create:

1. Use Excel to prepare content offline
2. Copy and paste into SharePoint forms
3. Use Quick Edit mode in the list for faster entry

### Coordinating with Others

1. **Content Calendar**: Maintain a shared calendar of upcoming announcements
2. **Review Process**: Have a peer review announcements before publishing
3. **Assign Ownership**: Designate someone to manage each announcement

## Troubleshooting

### My Announcement Isn't Showing

**Check these items**:
1. ✓ Today's date is between Valid From and Valid To
2. ✓ Valid From date is not in the future
3. ✓ Valid To date is not in the past
4. ✓ All required fields are filled in
5. ✓ The item is saved (not in draft)

**Solution**: Edit the item and verify all dates are correct

### My Image Isn't Displaying

**Common causes**:
1. ❌ Image URL is broken or incorrect
2. ❌ Image file was deleted
3. ❌ Permissions issue (image is private)
4. ❌ Invalid URL format

**Solutions**:
- Test the URL in a browser window
- Re-upload the image and copy the new URL
- Ensure image is in a publicly accessible location
- Use SharePoint-hosted images when possible

### The Celebration Icon Isn't Showing

**Check**:
1. The Celebration Icon field is set (not "None")
2. A Celebration Icon Position is selected
3. An Announcement Image is provided (icon overlays on image)

**Note**: Celebration icons only appear when an image is present

### Text is Hard to Read

**Issues**:
- Text color conflicts with background
- Overlay too dark/light

**Solutions**:
- Ask your administrator to adjust the overlay opacity
- Choose images with good contrast
- Use darker images if text is light-colored
- Use lighter images if text is dark-colored

### Too Many or Too Few Announcements Showing

**If too many**:
- Check Valid To dates and end outdated announcements
- Archive old items
- Spread announcements over different time periods

**If too few**:
- Verify Valid From dates aren't in the future
- Create more announcements
- Extend Valid To dates if appropriate
